## What makes these TLDs Different, and Why Should Financial Institutions Use Them?

.BANK and .INSURANCE are restricted top-level domains. You cannot register a .BANK or .INSURANCE domain unless you're a verified insurance or financial institution.

The registry performs verification before allowing registration of a domain. This creates immediate trust with your customers. When someone sees your .BANK or .INSURANCE domain, they know you've undergone verification, and know you meet specific industry requirements.

This exclusivity separates you from competitors using .COM or .NET domains. Anyone can register a .COM, but only verified institutions can register .BANK or .INSURANCE.

Using .BANK or .INSURANCE creates immediate brand differentiation. Customers recognize these TLDs the same way they recognize .EDU for educational institutions or .GOV for government agencies.

Your domain signals technical competence, demonstrating your commitment to security and customer protection while displaying that you've met verification requirements and maintain ongoing compliance.

.BANK domains are perfect for commercial banks, savings institutions, credit unions, investment firms, and payment providers, while .INSURANCE domains are made for insurance companies, brokers, and agents.

## How does DNSimple help comply with these security requirements?

Using .BANK and .INSURANCE domains comes with mandatory security requirements that are monitored for compliance continuously by the registry. Financial and insurance institutions must implement specific technical controls to protect their customers from phishing attacks, compromised transactions, and fraudulent emails. DNSimple's managed DNS service is fully compliant with all these security requirements, providing the technical infrastructure and tools you need to meet every registry mandate.

DNS Security Extensions, or DNSSEC is required to prevent DNS spoofing and cache poisoning attacks. Our interface simplifies DNSSEC configuration with one-click activation: just set it and we take care of the key management and signing automatically.

Multi-factor authentication is required for account access, protecting from unauthorized changes to your DNS configuration. DNSimple provides token-based two-factor authentication, as well as support for security keys. Using MFA, your users can have separate authentication credentials, enabling you to grant or remove access and permission levels without sharing passwords.

Email authentication is required, with implementations of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records to protect against email spoofing. DNSimple makes it straightforward to configure these records through our interface, API, or Terraform Provider.

SSL certificates are required for all web properties, meaning that your sites must use HTTPS with valid TLS certificates. DNSimple provides SSL certificate management, so you can order and install certificates directly through your DNSimple account while letting us handle renewals and updates automatically. Our platform is also HTTPS only, so all communications are always transmitted over encrypted connections.

Finally, you must use name servers that match your TLD. If you have a .BANK domain, you need .BANK name servers, and if you have a .INSURANCE domain, you need .INSURANCE name servers. DNSimple offers vanity name servers, so you can configure your own .BANK or .INSURANCE name servers with a few clicks. This meets the registry requirement while extending your branding to your DNS infrastructure.
